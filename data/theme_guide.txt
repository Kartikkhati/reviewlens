THEMES (copy the name exactly into my_theme)

Late delivery
    Order arrived much later than promised, or the delivery time kept increasing.
Delivery partner behaviour
    Rider was rude, asked for extra money, refused to come to the door, or marked the order delivered without delivering it.
Wrong or missing items
    Received the wrong dish, or some items in the order were missing.
Food quality & hygiene
    Food was cold, stale, spoiled, tasted bad, had small portions, or packaging leaked.
Pricing & fees
    High delivery, platform, packaging or surge fees, or menu prices higher than at the restaurant.
Payment problems
    Money deducted but order failed, charged twice, or charged an amount the user did not expect.
Refund issues
    Refund delayed, denied, partial, or given only as app wallet credit.
Order cancellation issues
    User could not cancel, was charged a cancellation fee, or the order was cancelled by the restaurant or app.
Customer support
    Only a chatbot, could not reach a human agent, complaint not resolved, or support was rude.
App bugs & usability
    App crashes, errors, slow loading, login or OTP problems, or wrong address or location.
Offers, coupons & memberships
    Coupons not applying, misleading discounts, or memberships like Swiggy One or Zomato Gold not giving promised benefits.
Praise: fast & convenient
    Positive review about quick delivery or easy ordering.
Praise: food & service quality
    Positive review about tasty food, good packaging or helpful service.
Praise: general
    General positive review without a specific reason.
Complaint: general
    Negative review with no specific reason, like 'worst app' or 'very bad service'.
Other
    Unrelated to the app experience, or impossible to judge (e.g. 'don't know yet').

TIE-BREAK RULES
- Several problems: pick the one the user is most upset about; if unclear, the one mentioned first.
- User asking where their money is -> "Refund issues", even if they also complain about support.
- Money deducted but order failed, or charged twice -> "Payment problems".
- Food arrived cold BECAUSE it was late -> "Late delivery".
- Rider being slow -> "Late delivery". Rider rude, asking for money, or faking delivery -> "Delivery partner behaviour".
- Vague negative review with no specific reason ("worst app", "very bad service") -> "Complaint: general".
- "Other" is ONLY for reviews unrelated to the app experience or impossible to judge (e.g. "don't know yet").
- Judge sentiment from the text, not the star rating.

SENTIMENT (my_sentiment): negative, neutral or positive - judged from the text, not the stars.
